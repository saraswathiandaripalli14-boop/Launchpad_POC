

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves per year.

Unused casual leaves cannot be carried forward to the next year.

A minimum of 24 hours’ notice is required to apply for casual leave, except in case of medical emergencies.

Sick leave is separate from casual leave and requires a medical certificate for absences exceeding three consecutive days.

Maternity leave will be granted as per applicable statutory regulations.

Paternity leave will be granted for a period of 15 days.
